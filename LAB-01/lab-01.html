Strawberry Pancakes

These fluffy strawberry pancakes are an easy and delicious breakfast treat topped with fresh strawberries.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup fresh strawberries
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, and baking powder together in a bowl.
  2. Add the milk, egg, and melted butter, then mix until combined.
  3. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat.
  4. Pour some batter into the pan and cook until bubbles form on the surface.
  5. Flip the pancake and cook the other side until golden brown.
  6. Serve the pancakes with fresh strawberries on top.
